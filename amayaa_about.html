Our Beginning

A Boutique Born from a Love of Handloom

Polka Dots began as a small boutique in Kolkata, built on the founder's lifelong passion for Indian textiles. What started as a personal collection of handloom sarees grew into a mission — to bring the finest weaves of India directly to women who value authenticity over mass production.

Amayaa, meaning “one without blemish” in Sanskrit, reflects our commitment to purity — of craft, of origin, and of purpose. Every saree we carry has been handpicked, verified for authenticity, and sourced with respect for the weaver communities that created it.

Our Philosophy

Why Handloom Matters

India's handloom sector employs over 35 lakh weavers — the second largest workforce in the country after agriculture. Yet handloom sarees account for a shrinking share of the market, displaced by power loom imitations that look similar but carry none of the craft.

At Amayaa, every purchase directly supports a weaver family. We pay fair prices, make advance purchases to reduce weaver risk, and document the origin story of every saree we sell. When you buy from us, you are not just wearing a saree — you are sustaining a tradition.

Our Promise to You

🏺
100% Authentic
Every saree is handpicked directly from weaver communities. No power loom imitations, ever. Silk Mark certified weaves are verified at source.
⚖
Weaver Fair Pay
We pay above-market rates to our weaver partners and make advance payments so they can buy raw materials without debt.
💎
Heritage Documented
