Shipping & Delivery Policy

We currently ship across India. International shipping is available on request — please WhatsApp us before placing an order.

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 2–3 business days of payment confirmation. As each saree is a handloom piece, orders placed during festive seasons may take an additional 1–2 days.

Delivery Timelines

  • Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad) — 4–6 business days
  • Other cities & towns — 6–9 business days
  • Remote / rural areas — 10–14 business days
  • International — 15–21 business days (on request)

Shipping Partners

We ship via reputed courier partners (Blue Dart, Delhivery, or India Post depending on your pincode). A tracking number will be shared via WhatsApp or email once your order is dispatched.

Shipping Charges

  • Free shipping on orders above ₹5,000
  • ₹120 flat shipping charge for orders below ₹5,000
  • International shipping charges calculated at checkout (contact us for a quote)

Packaging

Every saree is carefully folded, wrapped in tissue paper, and packed in our signature box to protect the fabric during transit. We do not use plastic wrapping in line with our sustainability commitment.

Section 02

Cancellation & Refund Policy

We take great care in curating authentic handloom pieces. Please read this policy carefully before placing your order.

Order Cancellation

  • Cancellations are accepted within 24 hours of placing the order, provided the item has not been dispatched
  • To cancel, WhatsApp us immediately with your order details
  • Orders that have already been dispatched cannot be cancelled
  • Custom or made-to-order sarees cannot be cancelled once production has begun

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns or exchanges within 7 days of delivery under the following conditions:

  • Item received is significantly different from what was described or shown
  • Item has a manufacturing defect or damage caused during transit
  • Wrong item was delivered

Returns are not accepted for:

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Minor colour variations due to screen settings (handloom colours may vary slightly from screen to actual)
  • Items that have been used, washed, or altered
  • Custom or personalised orders

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method. For COD orders, refunds are issued via bank transfer — please share your account details when raising a return request.

To raise a return or refund request, contact us within 7 days of delivery.
